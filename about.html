THE STORY BEHIND KALA'S KITCHEN

From Home Cooking to Kala's Kitchen

What started with cooking for family slowly grew into something everyone wanted to share.

HOW IT STARTED

A Kitchen Full of Love ❤️

♡ ✦ ♡

Kala started out just like any other mum, cooking meals for her family at home. But there was always something special about her food.

Her family quickly realised just how good her cooking was. Friends and family who tasted her food would always compliment it, and before long, people started joking that Kala should open her own restaurant.

What began as a family joke slowly became a real idea. Family and friends kept encouraging Kala to share her cooking with more people because they loved her food so much.

That is how the idea for Kala's Kitchen began: bringing the warmth and flavour of homemade South Indian food to other families.
