THE STORY BEHIND KALA'S KITCHEN

From Mum's Kitchen
to Kala's Kitchen

What started as a family joke became a little homemade food business, built around food that everyone already loved.

HOW IT STARTED

A love of cooking
became something more.

Before Kala's Kitchen was a business, Mum was simply a mum who loved cooking for her family.

Her food was always exceptional. We would often joke that she should open a restaurant because everyone loved her cooking so much.

Eventually, family and friends started encouraging her to actually turn the idea into something real. They loved her food and wanted to be able to enjoy it more often.

And that's where Kala's Kitchen began.
