OUR STORY

It all started with Mum's cooking.

Before Kala's Kitchen was a business, Mum was simply a mum who loved cooking for her family.

Her food was always exceptional. We would often joke that she should open a restaurant because everyone loved her cooking so much.

Eventually, family and friends started saying the same thing. They encouraged her to turn her cooking into something more, because they wanted to be able to enjoy her food more often.

That's how the idea of Kala's Kitchen began.
